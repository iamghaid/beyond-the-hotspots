About the data

  • Active cell-day: a 2 km grid cell with at least one qualifying detection on one local day. It counts burning activity, not individual fires.
  • Harmonized MODIS: combined Terra and Aqua cell-days × 1.84, an estimate on the VIIRS scale.
  • Qualifying detections: vegetation fire (type 0), MODIS confidence ≥ 30, VIIRS confidence nominal or high.
  • Status: observation coverage unverified; VIIRS hotspot type provisional.
  • Local time: America/Cuiaba.

Source: NASA FIRMS archive.
