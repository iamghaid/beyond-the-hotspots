Methods and results

How harmonized MODIS works

We count qualifying MODIS Aqua and Terra detections as unique active cell-days on a shared 2 km grid. The estimate averages the original 1.84× calibration with a calibration based on three MODIS fire-radiative-power bands (under 10, 10–50 and over 50 MW). Coefficients were fitted on February 2013 – January 2017 and compared with observed VIIRS (S-NPP) in the other periods.

  • 120 months outside fitting, 10 seasons: weighted absolute error 44.48% raw → 11.51% with 1.84× → 9.94% with the FRP ensemble.
  • 8 later seasons, Feb 2018 – Jan 2026: 12.75% with 1.84× → 10.79% across 96 months.
  • Why fire intensity: VIIRS detects small, weak fires that MODIS misses. When fires are intense, both sensors see them, so a single factor overestimates the largest fire months.
  • These numbers measure retrospective agreement between sensors, not ground-truth fire accuracy. The test periods were inspected during model development, so they are not a fresh independent test.
  • Weighted absolute error = sum of absolute differences ÷ total observed VIIRS activity, for the fixed rectangle.
  • Agreement is weaker in quiet weeks, at night, and with MODIS version 61.03. Coverage, fires on the ground and subarea performance are unverified.

About the data
  • Active cell-day: a 2 × 2 km equal-area cell with one or more qualifying detections on one local day. It counts detected burning activity, not individual fires, burned area or risk.
  • Qualifying detections: inferred vegetation fire (type 0), MODIS confidence ≥ 30, VIIRS nominal or high confidence. Local dates in America/Cuiaba.
  • Eras: February 2003 – January 2012 observed MODIS only. From February 2012, observed MODIS, observed VIIRS (S-NPP) and harmonized MODIS (FRP ensemble, VIIRS scale).
  • Status: satellite coverage unverified throughout. VIIRS hotspot typing is provisional. MODIS versions 6.03 and 61.03.
