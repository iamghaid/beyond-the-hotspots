Methods and results

How the estimate works

Combined Terra and Aqua MODIS activity × 1.84, fitted against observed S-NPP VIIRS on full weeks from 2013–2014 to 2016–2017.

  • 120 held-out months, 10 seasons: weighted absolute error 44.48% → 11.51%.
  • 8 later seasons, 2018–2026: 44.18% → 12.75% across 96 months.
  • Weighted absolute error = sum of absolute differences ÷ total observed S-NPP activity, for the fixed rectangle.
  • Agreement is weaker in quiet weeks, at night, and with MODIS version 61.03. Coverage, fires on the ground and subarea performance are unverified.

About the data
  • Active cell-day: a 2 × 2 km equal-area cell with one or more qualifying detections on one local day. It counts detected burning activity, not individual fires, burned area or risk.
  • Qualifying detections: inferred vegetation fire (type 0), MODIS confidence ≥ 30, S-NPP nominal or high confidence. Local dates in America/Cuiaba.
  • Eras: February 2003 – January 2012 observed MODIS only. From February 2012, observed MODIS, observed S-NPP VIIRS and the S-NPP-scale estimate.
  • Status: satellite coverage unverified throughout. S-NPP hotspot typing is provisional. MODIS versions 6.03 and 61.03.
